As autumn slowly sets in, we're still enjoying the memories of this year's fantastic rowing races. And what a great season we've had on home waters!

At the same time, we're already looking ahead to 2027. In exactly five months' time, it's here: the Head of the River Amstel 2027, taking place on 6 and 7 March 2027.

Are you looking for an exciting challenge on the winding Amstel, covering nearly 8 kilometres? Then take a look at these stunning images and make sure to register through your club's racing secretary so you can secure the best boat. This iconic long-distance classic is one race you won't want to miss in 2027! 🚣‍♀️🚣‍♂️
